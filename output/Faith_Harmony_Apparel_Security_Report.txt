EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Your business is vulnerable to common attacks.

IDENTIFIED WEAKNESSES & ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS:
-----------------------------------------------------------------

[!] Issue: No Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
    RISK: Hackers can easily access your accounts with just a password.

    ✅ SOLUTION: Enable MFA/2FA on ALL business accounts
    Steps:
    1. Go to your email settings (Gmail/Outlook) → Security → 2-Step Verification
    2. Download Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator app
    3. Enable MFA on banking, social media, and cloud storage accounts
    
    🛠️ TOOLS: Google Authenticator (Free), Microsoft Authenticator (Free)
    ⏱️ TIME: 30 minutes to set up all accounts
    💰 COST: Free
[!] Issue: No Reliable Backups
    RISK: Ransomware or hardware failure could delete all your business data.

    ✅ SOLUTION: Implement the 3-2-1 Backup Rule
    Steps:
    1. Use Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox for automatic file syncing
    2. Buy an external USB hard drive ($50-80) for weekly manual backups
    3. Enable 'Version History' in your cloud storage to recover old files
    
    🛠️ TOOLS: Google Drive (15GB Free), OneDrive (5GB Free), Backblaze ($7/month)
    ⏱️ TIME: 1 hour initial setup, then automatic
    💰 COST: Free to $10/month
[!] Issue: Weak or Reused Passwords
    RISK: If one account is breached, ALL your accounts are vulnerable.

    ✅ SOLUTION: Use a Password Manager
    Steps:
    1. Download Bitwarden (free) or 1Password (paid)
    2. Create a strong master password (12+ characters)
    3. Use the password generator to create unique passwords for each account
    4. Update your most important accounts first (email, banking)
    
    🛠️ TOOLS: Bitwarden (Free), 1Password ($3/month), LastPass (Free tier)
    ⏱️ TIME: 2 hours to migrate all passwords
    💰 COST: Free to $5/month
[!] Issue: No Employee Security Awareness
    RISK: Employees may click phishing links or fall for scams.

    ✅ SOLUTION: Basic Security Training
    Steps:
    1. Watch Google's 'Phishing Quiz' together (15 mins)
    2. Teach the '3 Rules': Don't click suspicious links, verify requests,
       report strange emails immediately
    3. Create a simple 1-page 'Security Cheat Sheet' for your office
    4. Review security tips in monthly team meetings
    
    🛠️ RESOURCES: Google Phishing Quiz (Free), FTC Cybersecurity Tips
    ⏱️ TIME: 1 hour initial training, 15 mins monthly refreshers
    💰 COST: Free
